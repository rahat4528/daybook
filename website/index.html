Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about the Vectis AI location intelligence platform.

How does Vectis locate street photos without GPS EXIF metadata? +

Our proprietary neural vision model extracts structural invariants: architectural facade rhythms, curb bevel geometries, street lighting fixtures, sun azimuth angle shadows, and overhead utility wiring layouts. These are cross-matched in high-dimensional vector space against our indexed corpus of 2,400+ metropolitan areas.

What is the typical search response latency? +

Standard street-level photo queries return ranked candidate coordinates within 250 to 500 milliseconds. Full planetary bounding box SAR radar change detection analyses typically complete in under 12 seconds.

Is our uploaded search data private and secure? +

Yes. Vectis enforces a strict zero-knowledge architecture. Uploaded images are converted to transient cryptographic embeddings in volatile memory and are immediately scrubbed. We never train public models on customer inputs, and enterprise plans support full on-premise air-gapping.

Can we export court-admissible evidence reports? +

All searches can be exported as cryptographic PDF evidence dossiers. Each dossier embeds full sensor telemetry, sun angle calculations, matching feature bounding boxes, and a SHA-256 digital signature compliant with international forensic admissibility standards.

How does the 14-day free trial work? +

You receive full access to the Professional tier with 500 test queries. No credit card is required to begin. At the end of 14 days, you can choose to subscribe or your account automatically shifts to a read-only archive.
